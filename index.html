About Us

A taxi service you can count on.

Skyshore Taxi Service is a taxi and transportation company based in Tangalle, on the southern coast of Sri Lanka. We drive local journeys, airport transfers, wedding functions and sightseeing trips, always with the same care.

Our aim is simple: pick you up on time, drive comfortably and courteously, and make sure you arrive safely at your destination.

  • Punctual pickups
  • Comfortable, well-kept vehicles
  • Courteous local drivers
  • Travel anywhere on the island
